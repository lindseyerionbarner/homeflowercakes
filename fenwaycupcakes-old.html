This holiday season, Homeflower Cakes is piloting a cupcake subscription program for our neighbors at Fenway Triangle!

We will be offering cupcake deliveries on the following dates:
Wednesday, Dec. 3rd: spiced carrot cake (cream cheese frosting)
Thursday, Dec. 11th: red velvet cake (cream cheese frosting)
Monday, Dec. 15th: hazelnut cream cake (chocolate buttercream)
Tuesday, Dec. 30th: vanilla orange curd cake (cinnamon buttercream)

Cupcakes are $2.50 each or two/$4. If you would like to sign up, fill out the form below to specify which days you would like to participate and how many cupcakes you would like to order!

Payment for your entire subscription is needed up front. We accept Venmo, Zelle, or cash, and will send you payment instructions after form submission. If you need to opt out of a delivery, send us a message 48 hours prior to delivery and we will refund you.

Deliveries will be made to your door around 8pm; we will message you with your provided contact info when your order has arrived. Let us know if you need a different delivery time. Residents who do not want deliveries left at their door may opt to pick up outside our unit in 1365 Boylston instead.
