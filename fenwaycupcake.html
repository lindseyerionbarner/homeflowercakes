This holiday season, Homeflower Cakes is piloting a cupcake subscription program for our neighbors at Fenway Triangle.

We will be offering cupcake deliveries on the following dates:
Wednesday, Dec. 3rd - spiced carrot (cream cheese frosting)
Thursday, Dec. 11th - red velvet (cream cheese frosting)
Monday, Dec. 15th- hazelnut cream (chocolate buttercream)
Tuesday, Dec. 30th- vanilla orange curd (cinnamon buttercream)

Cupcakes are $2.50 each.
If you would like to sign up, fill out the form below to specify which days you would like to participate and how many cupcakes you would like to order!

Additional info and terms

Payment for your entire subscription is required up front. We take venmo or cash. If you need to opt out of a delivery, you may request to do so 48 hours prior to delivery and we will refund you.

Deliveries will be made to your door between 7:30-8pm; we will message you with your provided contact info when your order has arrived. Residents who do not want deliveries left at their door may opt to pick up outside our residence (Unit 1248) instead.
